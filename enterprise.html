Exception intelligence

Know what went wrong—and what happens next.

Exception handling should be more than a warning on an invoice. DheePay Enterprise is intended to connect detection with ownership, action and resolution.

Matching

Price or quantity variance

Route to the appropriate buyer, receiver or authorised approver; support correction or an approved resolution path.

PO & receipt

Missing PO or goods receipt

Hold or route the invoice for the required reference, receipt confirmation or procurement review.

Data quality

Duplicate or invalid invoice data

Flag suspected duplicates and route incomplete or inconsistent data for review before further processing.

Tax & compliance

Tax or supplier issue

Send tax discrepancies or supplier-data concerns to the appropriate AP, tax or vendor-management owner.

Workflow

Overdue approval

Use configured reminders, ownership and escalation paths to surface work that is taking too long.

ERP posting

Posting failure

Record the failure, make it visible to the responsible team and support controlled reprocessing.

Exception rules, workflow actions and integration behaviour are configurable to the extent supported by the implemented product and agreed scope.

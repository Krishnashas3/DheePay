DHEEPAY LOGO — PRELIMINARY PUBLIC-WEB SIMILARITY REVIEW
Date: 25 October 2026

Scope
This is a preliminary public-web search for obvious visual-concept similarities. It is not a legal opinion, a comprehensive reverse-image search, a copyright ownership check, or trademark clearance. Search engines may omit unindexed, private, recently published, or registered marks.

Supplied concept reviewed
- Teal dual-loop / infinity-style emblem
- Central vertical stem intended to evoke lowercase d and p
- Electric sky-blue stem
- DheePay wordmark
- Green accent for Lite and gold accent for Enterprise

Preliminary assessment
- The general idea “D + P initials combined with an infinity symbol” is already present in multiple public logo listings. It is a crowded visual concept, so the supplied mark may have limited distinctiveness at the concept level.
- This search did not establish that the exact supplied artwork copies any one of these examples. Similarity of concept alone does not prove plagiarism or infringement; exact shapes, proportions, arrangement, source files, creation history, licenses and jurisdiction matter.
- The name “DheePay” should also be checked against “DePay” and phonetic/visual variants in the relevant goods/services classes, because both names can be encountered in a payments-related context. This is a screening flag, not a conclusion that a conflict exists.

Recommended before commercial launch
1. Search the Indian Trade Marks Registry for DHEEPAY, DHEE PAY, DHEEPAY LITE, DHEEPAY ENTERPRISE and phonetic variants, plus relevant wordmark and device/logo classes.
2. Ask an IP/trademark professional to perform a clearance search, including figurative/Vienna-code searches and relevant jurisdictions where you plan to sell.
3. Keep dated source files, design iterations, designer agreements and written assignment/licence terms proving your right to use the final artwork commercially.
4. Consider differentiating the emblem beyond the common infinity-DP concept before filing: use a more distinctive ledger/invoice geometry, negative-space construction or proprietary shape language, and review it against the identified examples.
